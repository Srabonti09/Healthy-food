# What is the healthiest food we can eat?

Lentils are an excellent choice. They provide plant-based protein and dietary fiber, are affordable, and can be used in soups, salads, and dishes such as dal.

A healthy diet includes a variety of foods. Vegetables, whole fruits, whole grains, and legumes each contribute different nutrients. A meal of lentils, brown rice, and vegetables is one simple way to bring several of these food groups together.
